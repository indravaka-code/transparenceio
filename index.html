About Transparence

A newly established securities lending solution built around a simple idea: pair institutional-grade technology with hands-on industry expertise.

Transparence was founded to answer a question many broker/dealers ask themselves: do we really need to build our own securities lending desk to participate in the market?

For most firms, the answer is no. Building a desk means recruiting specialized talent, licensing infrastructure, integrating data, and standing up the controls that regulators expect — all before earning the first dollar of revenue.

We built Transparence around the platform first — the outsourced service exists for firms that would rather not run it themselves.

Our platform covers the full securities lending lifecycle — from locates and trading execution to operations, reporting, and risk. For firms that want it fully managed, our team can also run that platform on your behalf as an outsourced front- and back-office operation.

The result is a single partner who can deliver the technology, and — when you want it — the people and operational discipline of a tier-one securities lending desk, sized to your business.
